Before We Get to the Science and Engineering…

Hello, and welcome to my tiny piece of the internet. By day (and unreasonable hours of the night), I am a Master’s student at KFUPM (ranked 63rd globally), where I research Double Half-Heusler thermoelectric materials for energy conversion.

In simple terms: I make materials, study them very closely, and try to understand how we might convince them to convert heat (ΔT) directly to electricity (ΔV).

Research Overview

Thermoelectric Materials

Nowadays (2024–date), I spend most of my time in the lab studying and researching advanced thermoelectric materials. I work under the supervision of Dr. Muath Mohammed Al-Malki (trained by Professor G. Jeffrey Snyder of Stanford and Northwestern University, USA).

My ongoing Master's thesis focuses on the Heusler class of TE materials, specifically Double Half-Heuslers. Our focus is on the Nb2Co2InSb and MgHfNi2Bi2 systems.

Energy System Modelling, Simulation, and Applied Machine Learning

Before thermoelectrics, my research journey began with models, data, and a lot of time behind my computer. I explored how energy systems could be optimised, designed conventional and renewable energy systems, and used machine-learning algorithms to tackle renewable-energy problems.

This area of research has yielded five first-author peer-reviewed publications. Read more in the Publications section.

Laboratory Research Activities

Bulk Thermoelectric Synthesis

We synthesize bulk thermoelectric materials by weighing and mixing stoichiometric amounts of starting powders under an argon atmosphere. The powder mixtures are cold-pressed, prereacted through heat treatment, and arc-melted. The melted ingots are then pulverized by ball milling and consolidated into dense bulk samples using spark plasma sintering.

Characterisation

We use powder XRD to investigate phase formation and crystal structure in both pulverised arc-melted ingots and sintered samples. SEM and EDS analyses are conducted to evaluate the microstructure, chemical composition, and elemental distribution of the consolidated materials.

Thermoelectric Performance Measurement

We conduct laser flash analysis, ZEM, Archimedes density measurement, and DSC, and estimate zT.

Then came graphic design. I taught myself the basics because it felt like the perfect companion to my newly found photography and videography craze. Before long, they became the kind of activities that make hours feel like minutes. They’re part of me now. I simply can’t let go!

Energy Policy Advocacy

Occasionally, I step beyond research to support advocacy efforts promoting a just energy transition and meaningful youth participation in energy decision-making.

Young people will live longest with the consequences of the energy choices being made today, so I believe our voices should be part of those conversations. Through youth-led organisations such as Youth in Natural Resources and Environmental Governance and the Strategic Youth Network for Development, I add my voice whenever and wherever I can.

Leadership and Volunteerism

For me, leadership has always been about showing up, taking responsibility, and helping whenever I can. My journey began at the University of Mines and Technology (UMaT), where I served in several roles from Campus Ambassador for Ghana Tertiary Minds, Executive Member of the Faculty of Engineering Students’ Association, Vice President of Enactus UMaT Students’ Club, President of the Ghana Muslim Students’ Association, and Member of the University Interdenominational Chaplaincy Board at UMaT.

I was recognised by the Deanship of Student Affairs as an Outstanding Executive Member for the 2025 service year. More than the recognition itself, the experience strengthened my ability to listen, collaborate, and work with people across different cultures - skills I continue to carry with me today.
